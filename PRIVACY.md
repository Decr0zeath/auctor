# Privacy policy

*Effective October 4, 2026*

Auctor collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads, and it never sends anything about you anywhere. This policy covers the Auctor browser extension. The Android app will be added here before it's released.

## What the extension reads

- **The address of the page**, on YouTube and Facebook only, to know which rules apply: the home feed, the Shorts player, a video page, and so on.
- **The page's layout**, to find and hide feeds, and to put the post or the prompt in their place.

It reads both on your device as the page loads. It doesn't keep them, and it never sends them anywhere. It doesn't read what you type, your messages, or your account details.

## What the extension stores

In your browser's local storage, on your device only:

- **Your settings:** what each feed is set to, and how long the wait and the pass last.
- **Changes waiting to take effect,** with the time each one applies, since changes that weaken protection wait 24 hours.
- **Passes:** when your time on a feed you chose to enter runs out.

Nothing else is stored. Your settings don't sync between devices. Export and import move them in a file that you save and open yourself. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

## Network requests

The extension makes none. The paintings and fonts in the post ship with it. One setting, **Thumbnails**, off by default, swaps the thumbnail a channel chose for a frame from the video. When it's on, the YouTube page loads that frame from YouTube's own image server, as it does any thumbnail. Nothing goes to us or to anyone else.

## Permissions

- **`storage`**, for the settings above.
- **Access to `www.youtube.com`, `www.facebook.com`, and `web.facebook.com`**, the only sites it changes. It runs on no other site.

## Sharing

There's nothing to share, so nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.
